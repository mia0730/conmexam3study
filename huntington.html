What supported what?

Steel bracing during excavation

The temporary steel bracing supported the excavation's retaining walls against lateral earth pressure and limited their movement — so the hole doesn't cave in while the foundation goes in.

The metal floor decking

The structural steel floor beams (or joists) supported the metal decking when it was installed. (Not the excavation bracing, not the excavation walls.)

Hook

"Hole, Skeleton, Floor." You have to make (and hold open) the hole before you can stand up the skeleton, and you need the skeleton before you can lay a floor on it.
