Masonry · Wall details · CMU lab · Wood · Framing · 525 Huntington

Course · Wythe · Joints L1

  • Course = horizontal row
  • Wythe = vertical layer 1 unit thick
  • Bed joint = horizontal · Head joint = vertical

Brick positions L1

  • Stretcher flat, long face horizontal
  • Header flat, short end out (ties wythes)
  • Soldier upright on end, long narrow face
  • Rowlock · Sailor (upright broad) · Shiner

Bonds L1

  • Flemish = H & S alternate within each course
  • English = whole courses alternate
  • Running · Common (header course ~every 6th) · Stack

CMU units & walls L2

  • Corner → wall ends / outside corners
  • Bullnose → rounded corners
  • Bond beam → horizontal rebar + grout
  • Partition: divides space, own weight only · Shear wall: resists in-plane lateral (wind/quake)

Mortar L3

  • Sand = bulk (limits shrinkage)
  • Lime = workability + water retention, bond
  • Cement = binder · water: as much as workable
  • Tooling compacts the joint → keeps water out
  • Types M S N O (MaSoN wOrK) strong→weak; use weakest that works
  • ⅜" joints standard · retemper ≤90 min, toss at 2½ hr

Brick making & movement L1·L4

  • Clay/shale → crush/screen → pug mill + water → mold → dry → fire in kiln
  • Kiln-dry brick absorbs moisture → expands
  • Brick grows → EXPANSION joint (compresses)
  • CMU shrinks → CONTROL joint
  • Joint = gap + backer rod + sealant

Cavity wall L4

  • Out→in: brick · cavity · rigid foam · CMU
  • Bears on foundation ledge (both wythes)
  • Water: cavity → flashing above lintel → weeps
  • Weeps = open head joints, 16–24" apart

Ties · shelf angles · panels L4

  • Adjustable tie: lateral support + aligns w/ veneer joint (not weight!)
  • Other backup: sheathed wood studs (or metal studs)
  • Shelf angle every 1–2 stories; gap below = horiz. joint
  • Sandwich panel: masonry · insulation · masonry (concrete · foam · concrete)

CMU lab L5

  • 6" nominal · ⅜" joints · concave
  • Ladder/truss reinf. in horizontal bed joints
  • 16" o.c. = every 2nd course (8" courses)
  • 3 modular brick courses = 1 CMU (8")

Wood & lumber L6

  • Soft/hard = tree groups (conifer vs broadleaf), not hardness
  • Cedar heartwood resists decay → outdoors
  • Plain sawn: parallel cuts, tangent to rings (framing)
  • Kiln: controlled heat, humidity, airflow (days) · Air: ambient, slower
  • Green = MC > 19% · S4S after drying

Sizes & products L6·L7

  • 1×6 = ¾×5½ · 2×8 = 1½×7¼ · 8×8 = 7½×7½
  • Rule: 1→¼ less · 2–6→½ less · >6→¾ less
  • Veneer grade = size & number of defects
  • Sheathing: plywood & OSB
  • I-joist web OSB → manufacturer's span tables
  • SIP = OSB + foam core · Dovetail = tapered interlock

Framing & roofs L8·L9

  • Platform studs = 1 story
  • Joists span short way · sheathing perpendicular to joists
  • Gable: 2 slopes at a ridge · ridge beam holds upper rafter ends
  • Bird's mouth sits on top plate · sheathing before covering
  • 525 Huntington: braced excavation → steel → deck. Bracing held excavation walls; steel beams held deck.
